About

I'm a Principal Scientist at Adobe, where I work on agentic AI for Adobe Experience Platform (AEP). Most recently I've been one of the architects of AEP Agent Orchestrator, Adobe's multi-agent platform built on open protocols, and before that I worked on the AI behind several generations of AEP AI Assistant.

I joined Adobe in 2017 as a data scientist. My early years were in Adobe Target, working on recommendations, contextual bandits and A/B-testing statistics. Later I brought anytime-valid confidence sequences into production experimentation in Adobe Journey Optimizer.

Before that I was a theoretical physicist. I did my PhD in Physics at Stanford, working on strongly correlated electron systems and the interface between theory and experiment in high-temperature superconductors.

What I like most is having enough theory to read the papers and enough engineering to ship them.

Selected work

  • AEP Agent Orchestrator · 2025–present
    Took a decentralized multi-agent platform from zero to one, built on the open Agent2Agent (A2A) protocol. My work covered the reasoning engine for multi-step planning and execution, multi-tenant configuration, A2A protocol extensions, and the developer docs that agent-building teams across Adobe use.
  • Adobe Summit 2025 Strategy Keynote · 2025
    Helped shape the agentic AI vision for Adobe Experience Cloud and demoed the first Agent Orchestrator prototype live on stage.
  • AEP AI Assistant · 2023–2025
    Worked on core AI from pre-alpha through GA and beyond. I designed the AI-quality framework: conversation-log collection, data labeling, error analysis, model benchmarking, and an in-house annotation platform.
  • Summit Concierge · 2025
    Led AI quality for a consumer-facing conversational assistant in the Adobe Summit mobile app.
  • Anytime-valid experimentation · 2022–2023
    Led the empirical research that brought anytime-valid confidence sequences (safe "peeking" in A/B tests) to Adobe Journey Optimizer (docs, paper).
  • Adobe Target · 2017–2022
    Applied ML and statistics for personalization: recommendations, contextual bandits and A/B-test methodology.
