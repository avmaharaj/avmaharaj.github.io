About

I'm a Principal Scientist at Adobe working on agentic AI. Lately, that's the harness behind Adobe CX Enterprise Coworker. Before that, it was the runtime and evaluation of AEP AI Assistant.

I joined Adobe in 2017 as a data scientist on Adobe Target, working on recommendations, bandits and A/B tests. Later I worked on letting people peek at running experiments without fooling themselves, which became anytime-valid confidence sequences in Journey Optimizer.

Before that I was a theoretical physicist. I finished my PhD at Stanford in 2017, with a thesis on various topics related to high-temperature superconductors.

I like knowing enough theory to read the papers and enough engineering to ship them. This site is where I write about both, mostly stats and AI/ML.

Selected work

  • Adobe CX Enterprise Coworker · 2025–now
    A coding-agent-style harness for marketing automation. It grew out of AEP Agent Orchestrator, a distributed multi-agent orchestrator built on open protocols (A2A). Docs.
  • AEP AI Assistant · 2023–2025
    Core AI and its evaluation loop, from pre-GA onward. The evaluation side is written up here.
  • Anytime-valid experimentation · 2022–2023
    Confidence sequences that make it safe to peek at A/B tests, from research through to production in Journey Optimizer. The statistics are written up here, and the shipped version is in the product docs.
  • Adobe Target · 2017–2022
    Recommendations, contextual bandits and A/B-test statistics for personalization.
